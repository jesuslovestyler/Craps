How it works & payouts

You are the shooter. A round has two phases. On the come-out roll, a 7 or 11 is a “natural,” 2, 3, or 12 is “craps,” and any other total (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) becomes the point. In the point phase you keep rolling: the point wins, a 7 (“seven-out”) ends the round, and everything else just rolls again.

The bets

  • Pass Line — pays 1:1. Bet before the come-out. Wins on a natural (7/11), loses on craps (2/3/12). If a point is set, wins when the point repeats before a 7. Once the point is set, a Pass bet cannot be taken down (that is a real craps rule, enforced here).
  • Don’t Pass — pays 1:1. The mirror image: loses on 7/11, wins on 2 or 3, and 12 pushes (your stake comes back). In the point phase it wins on a 7 and loses if the point repeats. Like Pass, it is locked in once the point is set at this table.
  • Odds — true odds, up to 3× the flat bet. Once a point exists, you may add an Odds bet behind your Pass bet (or behind a moved Come bet, using its “+ Odds” button). Odds pay the true mathematical odds with no house edge: point 4 or 10 pays 2:1, 5 or 9 pays 3:2, 6 or 8 pays 6:5. Odds can be taken down at any time.
  • Come — pays 1:1. Placed during the point phase. The very next roll is that bet’s own come-out: 7 or 11 wins, 2/3/12 loses, and any other total becomes that bet’s own number — it then wins if its number repeats before a 7. A Come bet is a contract bet and cannot be taken down once placed (its odds can). Two honest house rules here: (1) a Come bet placed just before a seven-out roll still wins on that 7, because 7 is a natural for the new bet even as it ends the Pass round; (2) on the come-out roll of a new Pass round, existing Come bets stay working for the flat bet (a 7 loses them, their number repeating wins them) but their odds are OFF — odds neither win nor lose on that roll, and are handed back if the flat bet settles.
  • Field — one roll. Wins if the next roll is 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 11, or 12. Pays 1:1, except 2 and 12 pay 2:1. Loses on 5, 6, 7, 8.
  • Hardways — Hard 4 & 10 pay 7:1; Hard 6 & 8 pay 9:1. A hardway wins when its pair rolls (3+3 for Hard 6, and so on) before a 7 or the “easy” version of the same number. Hardways are always working, including on the come-out. When a hardway hits, the profit is paid and the bet stays up riding; take it down any time with its ✕ button.
  • Place 6 & Place 8 — pay 7:6 (rounded to the nearest chip). The bet wins a payout each time its number rolls before a 7 during the point phase, and the bet itself stays up. Place bets are OFF on the come-out roll by default — a come-out 7 or the number itself neither wins nor loses them — unless you turn them on with the toggle on the table.

Good to know

  • Tap a spot with a chip selected to add that chip to the bet. The ✕ on a spot takes the bet down and returns your chips, where the rules above allow it.
  • Press Space to roll. Starting stack is 1,000 play chips; when you are broke and nothing is on the table, a Fresh stack button appears.
  • Your balance, bets, and stats are remembered on this device between visits. Play chips only — no real money, ever.
